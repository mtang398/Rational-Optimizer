# Read First

The active question is whether **Factorized Every-Step Robust Finite-Difference
Gradient-Ledger Muon, version 1** retains its established loss advantage under
exact M0 and M1 transfer while keeping matched total time approximately equal
to the strongest control.

Read the artifact in this order:

1. `RLB_OPTIMIZER_FAIRNESS_CONTRACT.md` for locked LR/WD and comparison rules.
2. The active transfer package under `experiments/` for source-cell provenance,
   manifest, launch state, and reports.
3. `optimizer_design/` for the complete method and scalability audit.
4. Raw JSONL files for endpoint and timing evidence.

Historical endpoints guide admission but do not establish new transfer claims.
Only completed matched endpoints count. M0 is compared with AdamW and M1 with
Muon. Every non-method hyperparameter is inherited exactly from the original
control cell.
