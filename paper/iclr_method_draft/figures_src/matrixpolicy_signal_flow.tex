% Step 1 design document.
% Domain / format / density: ICLR method figure, TikZ standalone, medium density.
% Five-second message: detached summaries are produced above the stop-gradient
% boundary, collected on a read-only bus, and consumed by three MatrixPolicy
% controls. Gradients move through the update operators, while the current
% matrix pair is read and mutated only at the final apply/rescale stage.
% Layout: centered producers at top; summary bus and control cards in the
% policy band; two lower lanes separate gradient/update flow from matrix-state
% flow. Arrows terminate on named cards, boxes, or bus edges.
% Visual hazards to avoid: no comb of dashed arrows, no text on arrow shafts,
% no title/formula overlap inside producer cards, no dangling route endpoints.
