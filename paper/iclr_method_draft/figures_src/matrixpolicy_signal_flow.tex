% Step 1 design document.
% Domain / format / density: ICLR method figure, TikZ standalone, medium density.
% Five-second message: detached summaries are produced above the stop-gradient
% boundary, collected on a read-only bus, and consumed by exactly three
% MatrixPolicy operations; AdamW-only tensors bypass MatrixPolicy entirely.
% Layout: producers at top, one detached-summary bus, three consumer cards,
% then the matrix-pair update path. Arrows are vertical/horizontal and every
% arrow terminates on a named card, bus, or operation.
% Visual hazards to avoid: no comb of dashed arrows, no text on arrow shafts,
% no title/formula overlap inside producer cards, no dangling route endpoints.
